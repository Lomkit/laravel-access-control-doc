title: What is public in 1.x
description: The releases of 1.x follow semantic versioning for what this documentation describes. Learn what that covers, and what may change in any release.
---

The releases of 1.x follow semantic versioning for what this documentation and the [upgrade guide](https://github.com/Lomkit/laravel-access-control/blob/1.x/UPGRADE.md) describe, and do not mark `@internal`.

## Covered

- The classes, and their public and protected methods, that the documentation shows an application calling, extending, implementing or overriding.
- The exception classes and the classes they extend, thrown in the cases the documentation gives. Their messages, and the way they are built, are not covered.
- The refusal codes of the policies, `unsaved`, `no_perimeter`, `outside_boundary`, `not_found` and `outside_rows`, and the names of the search attributes, `_access` and `_boundary`.
- The artisan commands, their options, their exit codes and the JSON of `access:check`. A minor release may add problems and notes to `access:check`, so a CI that passed may fail after it, as with the new rules of a linter.
- The assertions of `InteractsWithAccess`, whose signatures do not change in 1.x. A minor release may add checks to them, so a test that passed may fail after it, when the new check reveals a disagreement.
- `Access::searchTokens()` and `Access::guardSearch()`, with the bodies the guard accepts and the parameters it forces. A release, even a patch, may refuse a parameter or a filter that turns out to widen what a reader finds: the guard fails closed rather than keep an opening.
- `Access::indexKeysOf()`, `Access::indexReaderKeys()`, `IndexReaderKeys` with its `model()`, `isClosed()` and `values()`, and the `assertIndexKeysAgree()` assertion, with the behaviour that [Your own search client](/search/your-own-client) gives: both fields for each document and each reader, digests of 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters, never an empty list for an open reader nor in the `_access` of a document, and, for a closed reader, one value in each field that is not a digest and that no document carries. The values themselves change as the keys do.

## The classes you extend

Your application extends `Perimeter`, `Control` and `Boundary`. A minor release may add methods to them, and a method of the same name in your subclass would then clash: **give the methods you add to your subclasses distinctive names.** Their covered members:

| Class | Covered members |
| --- | --- |
| `Perimeter` | `new()`, `allowed()`, `should()`, `query()`, `searchKeys()`, `reindexWhen()`, `unrestricted()`, `admits()` and `checksInMemory()`; the protected hooks `allows()`, `includes()` and `scope()` |
| `Control` | `new()`, `abilityFor()`, `decide()`, `applies()` and `reachableKeys()`; the protected `perimeters()`, `boundary()` and `applicablePerimeters()`, and the protected `$model` property, declared by its default value and never assigned elsewhere |
| `Boundary` | `where()`, `none()` and `checksInMemory()`; the protected hooks `scope()`, `includes()`, `searchKeys()` and `admits()`; `readOnlyWhenNotNull()` on the boundary that `where()` returns |

`ControlledPolicy` gets no new method in 1.x, since the methods of your policy are named after your abilities: it keeps the seven standard abilities, the protected `decideByControl()` and `declaredControl()`, and nothing else.

## Not covered

- A class or a method marked `@internal` may change or disappear in any release, whatever its PHP visibility, and so may a public or protected method that the documentation does not describe. Do not call, extend, implement, override or type against them.
- The classes behind the artisan commands, the control registry, and the search keys and filters, such as `SearchKeyValues`, `SearchDocuments`, `SearchRules` and `SearchFilter`, are internal.
- `AccessServiceProvider` is registered by package discovery: list it in `bootstrap/providers.php` if your application turns the discovery off, and do not extend it.
- Calling the protected hooks of another perimeter from a perimeter class is not supported: call [`admits()`](/digging-deeper/row-checks#which-perimeter-grants-a-row).
- The access keys, and their digests in your search indexes, are not an API. They live in your indexes, though: a release that changes them says so in its upgrade notes, with the reindex it needs, even a minor one.

`ControlledSearchBuilder` is the builder that `search()` returns on a `ControlledSearchable` model: its `controlled()`, `controlledFor()` and `uncontrolled()` are public.
