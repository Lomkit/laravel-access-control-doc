## Upgrading to 1.0 from 0.x

Version 1.0 requires PHP 8.3 and Laravel 13. Applications that stay on Laravel 12 can use version 0.7, the last 0.x release.

The [upgrade guide on GitHub](https://github.com/Lomkit/laravel-access-control/blob/1.x/UPGRADE.md) lists every breaking change of 1.0, area by area: queries, perimeters, boundaries, parents, search, policies, abilities and the laravel-rest-api extension. Go through the sections of the features that your application uses. The table at its end maps each removed call to its replacement.

### Coming from 0.6

Version 0.7 already changed several things. If you upgrade from 0.6, apply the [0.7.0 checklist](https://github.com/Lomkit/laravel-access-control/releases/tag/v0.7.0) first, then the 1.0 guide.
